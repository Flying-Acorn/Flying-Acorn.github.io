Number Chain 2248 – a number puzzle game for iOS and Android by Flying Acorn

زنجیره اعداد: 2248 (Number Chain 2248) is a free mobile number puzzle game. Connect matching number blocks to merge them into bigger numbers, and keep chaining merges to reach 2248 and beyond. The game is designed for Persian speakers.

What you can do in the game

  • Chain and merge – link matching number blocks to combine them into higher numbers.
  • Global leaderboard – compare your best score with other players.
  • Weekly leaderboard – a fresh ranking every week.

Accounts and your data

You can play without creating an account. Optionally, you can link your Google account or Apple ID from the profile screen. The only purpose of linking is to back up your game progress and restore it when you change or reinstall your device. When you link a Google account, the game asks only for your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) and uses it only to identify your saved progress. It is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Details of what the game collects, how it is used and how to have it deleted are in the privacy policy.
