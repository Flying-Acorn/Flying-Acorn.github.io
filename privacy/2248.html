This policy explains what information the زنجیره اعداد: 2248 (Number Chain 2248) mobile game collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can have it deleted. In short:

  • You can play without an account. The game creates an anonymous player account for your device.

  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Your game account and game data

When you first open the game, it creates an anonymous player account on our servers. Linked to that account we store:

  • a random player identifier and a public player ID (shown in the game's settings);
  • the player name and avatar you choose in the game;
  • your game data: scores, highest numbers reached, in-game currency and items, and a record of your in-app purchases;
  • your leaderboard scores.

Your player name, avatar and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards.

If you link a Google account or an Apple ID, the game also keeps a backup copy of your game progress on our servers, so that it can be restored on another device. Without a linked account, your progress is kept only on your device.

Device and app information

To run the game and our services, the game sends us technical information about your device and installation: device model and name, a device identifier generated by the operating system, operating system and graphics hardware details, app version, the store you installed the game from, your time zone and the number of sessions played. From the IP address of your connection we determine your approximate country, which we use to route you to the right server and to show region-appropriate content. We do not collect your precise location.

Google account information (only if you choose to sign in with Google)

You can link a Google account from the profile screen. The game then requests only the openid, email and profile permissions, and we receive and store:

  • your Google account identifier;
  • your email address;
  • your name (first name, last name and display name);
  • the web address of your profile picture.

We do not request access to any other Google data, such as your contacts, files, calendar or Google Play activity.

Apple account information (only if you choose to sign in with Apple)

If you link an Apple ID, we receive your Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address) and your name.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the app store you use (Google Play or the App Store). We receive the purchase and transaction details needed to confirm the purchase and deliver the item. We never receive your payment card or bank details.

Information collected by our analytics and advertising partners

The game includes software from the partners listed in the section "Who we share information with". They collect usage events (for example levels played and ads watched), crash reports, device information, your IP address, and advertising identifiers (the Android Advertising ID or, on iOS if you allow tracking, the IDFA). If you allow notifications, a push notification token is also collected.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game: keep your progress, deliver purchases, run the leaderboards, and send notifications you have allowed.
  • To back up and restore your progress across devices when you link a Google or Apple account.
  • To support you: answer your questions and restore or correct your game data on request.
  • To improve the game: understand how the game is played, fix crashes and balance the game, using analytics.
  • To show ads, which keep the game free, and to measure where our players come from.
  • To keep the game fair and secure: prevent cheating, fraud and abuse.

3. How we use Google user data

The Google account information described above is used only to identify your account, so that your saved game progress can be found and restored when you sign in again, including on another device, and to show in the game which account is linked.

  • It is not used for advertising, including personalised or retargeted ads.
  • It is not sold and it is not shared with our analytics or advertising partners.
  • It is not used to build a profile of you, to determine creditworthiness, or to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Our staff read it only when you ask us for support, when needed for security, or when required by law.

Flying Acorn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the following service providers, which process it on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Analytics and crash reporting: Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging, GameAnalytics and AppMetrica.
  • Advertising: CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions) and the ad networks it works with, which may include Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Mintegral, InMobi, Liftoff, DT Exchange, Bigo Ads and Yandex Ads.
  • App stores (Google Play, App Store) to process purchases.
  • Sign-in providers (Google, Apple) when you choose to sign in with them.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our players, our company or others. If our business is transferred to another company, your information may be transferred with it under this policy.

5. Advertising choices

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings. On iOS, the game asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

6. How we store and protect information

Your game account, game data and linked account information are stored on servers operated by Flying Acorn. All communication between the game and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to player data is limited to the staff who need it to run and support the game. Depending on where you play, your information may be processed on servers outside your country. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

7. How long we keep information, and deleting it

  • We keep your game account and game data and linked account information for as long as your account exists, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • You can unlink your Google or Apple account at any time from the profile screen.
  • To delete your account and all information we hold about it, including your linked Google or Apple account information and your saved progress, email support@flyingacorn.studio from the email address of your linked account, or include the public player ID shown in the game's settings. We will delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep some information to meet a legal obligation.
  • You can also remove the game's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections, or from your Apple ID settings under "Sign in with Apple".
  • Our analytics and advertising partners keep the data they collect according to their own retention policies, linked above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To use these rights, contact us at support@flyingacorn.studio. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us at support@flyingacorn.studio and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date at the top of this page, and if we change how the game uses Google user data or other personal information in a significant way, we will tell you in the game before the change takes effect.
